Gun Shakers is a mod of Quake 4 that is supposed to turn this first person shooter into a top-down arcade shooter.

There are 4 unique weapons, but you get to mix pairs together to make a new weapon out of the pair combined. (You can only hold 2 weapons)

The enemies you fight will spawn in waves.

After successfully clearing a wave of enemies, you gain the ability to shop for new weapons and special abilities.

Whatever you buy will substitute a weapon or ability you had previously.

There are 5 abilities to choose from. (You can only use one ability)

A Help Screen is present on the main menu, and the pause screen of Quake 4 if you at any time forget how the mod should be played.
